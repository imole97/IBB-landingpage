Introduction
"We didn't set out to decorate rooms. We set out to understand people — and let the rooms follow."

I started Interiors by B because I genuinely believe that where you live and work should feel like you — not like a showroom, not like a trend, and definitely not like someone else's idea of nice.

Every project we take on starts the same way: with a conversation. I want to know how you use your space, what bothers you about it, what you love — and honestly, what you've been dreaming about but haven't said out loud yet.

Your space should tell your story. We're just here to help you write it well.

The Index

The Index Questions answered.

Everything you need to know before we begin.

What does working with IBB look like? +
It starts with a conversation — a real one. We want to understand you, your space, and what you're hoping to feel when you walk through the door. From there we take you through concept development, design and detailing, sourcing, and delivery. We stay involved at every stage.
Do you work outside Nigeria and the UK? +
Yes. Our studio works across both Nigeria and the UK, and our e-design service means we can work with clients anywhere in the world. Location has never stopped a good story from being told.
I have a smaller budget — can IBB still help? +
Absolutely. Our e-design service was built specifically to make intentional, considered design accessible at every budget level. You get the same care and approach as our full service, delivered online.
How long does a full project typically take? +
It depends on the scope. As a guide, most residential projects run between three and six months from brief to reveal. We'll give you a clear timeline at the start of your project and keep you updated throughout.
What's included in an e-design package? +
A tailored mood board, a spatial layout plan, a curated sourcing list with product recommendations, and a design direction document you can take forward yourself or hand to a contractor. Delivered digitally, specific to your space.
How do I know if I'm ready to start? +
If you've been thinking about your space — what's bothering you, what you wish it felt like, what you've been putting off — you're probably ready. You don't need to have all the answers before we speak.
